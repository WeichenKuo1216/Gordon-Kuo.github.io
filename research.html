[Undergraduate Thesis] Beyond Terminal Punctuality: Evaluating the Effect of Railway Corporatization in Taiwan

  • Evaluated TRC punctuality following its 2024 corporatization using monthly 2021–2025 data and a difference-in-differences comparison with THSRC; estimated a 1.50 percentage-point relative decline in official punctuality.
  • Assessed robustness using Newey–West HAC standard errors, false-treatment-date placebo tests, and leave-one-month-out sensitivity analysis.
  • Constructed a station-stop-exposed punctuality measure and identified a 14.4 percentage-point gap between official terminal-based punctuality and passenger-exposed punctuality.
