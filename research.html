[Undergraduate Thesis] Beyond Terminal Punctuality: Evaluating the Effect of Railway Corporatization in Taiwan

• Evaluated TRC punctuality following its 2024 corporatization using monthly 2021–2025 data and a benchmark difference-in-differences comparison with THSRC; estimated a 1.50 percentage-point relative decline in official punctuality.

• Assessed robustness using Newey–West HAC standard errors, false-treatment-date placebo tests, and leave-one-month-out sensitivity analysis; coefficient estimates remained negative and pre-treatment placebo effects were insignificant.
